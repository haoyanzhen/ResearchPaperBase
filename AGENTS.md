## Main aim

Accomplish a web app as a deep search & research scholar base.

- Can generate proper search terms for downloading scholar papers and well parse & save them into database, with daily push to users' email.
- Can make graph-RAG based on paper database and interact with users.
- Can make literature review on a research topic based on paper database.
- Support multiple users to share a scholar paper dataset and viewpoint.
